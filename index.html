𝄞 Air Violin

    
Play a violin in the air. Your webcam watches your arms and hands and the game plays the notes.

    

    
    
    
      
  • Left hand up by your left shoulder, palm facing the camera. Fold fingers down to play higher notes.
    • 
      
  • Right elbow high or low picks the string: high = G (lowest notes), low = E (highest).
    • 
      
  • Right hand bows side to side. Faster = louder.
    • 
    

    

    
On a phone: turn it sideways and prop it up about 1.5 m away so your upper body fits in the picture.

    

      
      
    

    
🔒 Your video never leaves this device. Nothing is uploaded, recorded or saved.

How to play

    

      

        
Notes in D major

        

        
Press and hold a note in the table to hear it.

        

      

      

        
Your body

        
    
          
  • String: right elbow high = G string … low = E string.
    • 
          
  • Note: left hand open = open string. Fold index, middle, ring, little finger = 1, 2, 3, 4 (thumb doesn't count).
    • 
          
  • Bow: move your right hand side to side. Speed = loudness. Change direction for a new stroke. Change fingers while bowing to slur.
    • 
          
  • Vibrato: a small quick wobble of your left hand.
    • 
        

        
Keyboard (no camera needed)

        
    
          
  • G D A E pick the string
    • 
          
  • 0–4 fingers down (0 = open)
    • 
          
  • Hold Space to bow (each press changes direction), or ←/→
    • 
          
  • Hold Shift to bow louder, V for vibrato
    • 
          
  • H help, Esc close
    • 
          
  • Or press and hold a key on the piano strip to hear that note
    •
